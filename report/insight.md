# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Key Insights

1. The platform relies heavily on new customers  
   - The majority of GMV is generated by new users  
   - Repeat purchase rate is very low (~3%)  
   → Customer retention is weak  

2. Delivery performance is a key driver of customer satisfaction  
   - Late deliveries reduce average review scores significantly (4.3 → 2.6)  
   - Late orders also have a much higher proportion of low ratings  

3. Revenue is concentrated among a small number of sellers  
   - Approximately 17.5% of sellers generate 80% of total GMV  
   - The platform shows a strong long-tail structure  

4. Platform growth is driven by order volume rather than higher spending  
   - Order count increases over time  
   - AOV remains relatively stable  

5. Product categories exhibit different revenue patterns  
   - Some categories are volume-driven  
   - Others rely on higher average prices  

---

## Recommendations

1. Improve customer retention  
   - Introduce loyalty programs or membership systems  
   - Use targeted promotions to encourage repeat purchases  
   - Implement personalized recommendations  

2. Optimize delivery performance  
   - Improve logistics and reduce delivery delays  
   - Provide proactive communication for late deliveries  
   - Consider compensation mechanisms (e.g., coupons)  

3. Reduce dependency on top sellers  
   - Support mid-tier sellers with better exposure  
   - Provide incentives to improve seller performance  
   - Diversify seller base to reduce risk  

4. Focus on sustainable growth  
   - Balance customer acquisition with retention strategies  
   - Encourage repeat purchases to increase customer lifetime value  

5. Optimize product strategy  
   - Invest more in high-performing categories  
   - Identify categories with high potential for growth
